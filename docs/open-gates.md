# Spinivo release gates and evidence

Spinivo `1.0.0b1` was published as a limited preview under decisions 7 and 17. Decision 21 released `1.1.0` as a regular release under decision 20. Decision 23 releases `1.2.0`, which adds the printable HTML assessment report, under the same rules. Decision 20 supersedes the release requirements for outside data, independent validation and baseline-shape evidence in decisions 5, 7, 13 and 14. No new third-party data, measurements or external sign-off are required for software release. The owner chooses the version label and authorises publication. Decision 17 approved the preview only. Package index publication needs separate approval. [docs/decisions.md](decisions.md), [docs/plan.md](plan.md)

## Development evidence and assay limits

- **Real integral labels.** Report scores and counts by cause as development evidence. Use decision 3's predeclared, sourced evidence. The internal LLM jury supplies development annotations. Keep unresolved labels indeterminate. Human expert review is optional. The real pilot has zero independently established reliable regions. The widened FID05 window has an unreliable label for a separate band and returns **cannot be assessed**. The Bruker 7.64–7.69 ppm window lost its frozen baseline and bounds causes. The regional capture flag differs from the pair-only rubric rule. Both findings await owner review. [docs/integral-rubric.md](integral-rubric.md), [tools/compare_references.py](../tools/compare_references.py)
- **Baseline.** The `pilot-3` warning uses `max(P, C) > 0.017` for sensitivity to spike position and baseline placement. Its independent validation remains open. Text and JSON report baseline-shape sensitivity of the area ratio as a diagnostic outside the checked-purity budget. Decision 20 allows release with this evidence gap. [docs/decisions.md](decisions.md), [docs/integral-rubric.md](integral-rubric.md), [src/spinivo/assessment_report.py](../src/spinivo/assessment_report.py)
- **Nishizaki comparisons.** Publish the non-independent development results for both methods with every miss. Workbook formulas and intermediate quantities test reproducibility. Current pilot misses against workbook area ratios are FID02 I149/H146 +1.08%, FID06 H5/F4 −1.74% and FID06 I5/F4 −1.12%, expressed as relative ratio differences. Compare calculated caffeine mass fractions separately with the 99.9% certificate in mass-fraction percentage points. For example, the FID02 workbook's external and same-tube calculations give 99.34% and 99.94% on that basis, differences of −0.56 and +0.04 percentage points. [docs/pilot-pairs.md](pilot-pairs.md), [tools/compare_references.py](../tools/compare_references.py)
- **External transfer.** Decision 2 applies to each external assay. Require a traceable pair record or documented analyst attestation, separate receiver gain and scale evidence, and justified response transfer with its uncertainty. The pilot still lacks applicable T1 and verified pulse calibration, tube fill, receiver scaling, quantitative recovery and residual transfer uncertainty. Those gaps limit checked results on the affected assays. [docs/decisions.md](decisions.md), [docs/pilot-pairs.md](pilot-pairs.md)

## Optional holdout study

Decisions 13 and 19 define the optional protocol. All current manifest cases are development cases. The prior search found no qualified deposit, and a Bruker same-tube set with an independent reference is still missing. Before reporting decision 19 results, the scorer must separate shared-lot covariance from independent preparation counts. [docs/decisions.md](decisions.md), [data/case_manifest.json](../data/case_manifest.json), [src/spinivo/accuracy.py](../src/spinivo/accuracy.py)

| Study measure | Status | Decision 13 criterion as amended by decision 19 |
|---|---|---|
| Independent holdout | No roster | Freeze a roster from a separate public deposit or purpose-prepared public set with an independent purity reference and at least eight independent scored preparations per method. Each preparation has its own weighings, solutions and tube. Count shared weighings, stock solutions or calibrant solutions as one. A shared certified lot preserves separate preparation counts. Include its certificate uncertainty in each error uncertainty and count it once in pooled results. Merge aliases and repeats. Screen for exposed or potentially shared development material, out-of-scope experiments and missing independent references before freezing. Keep flagged cases, missing inputs, failed runs and large errors on the frozen roster. |
| Internal-standard accuracy | Unmeasured | Average repeats within each preparation and weight preparations equally. Report signed bias and require a median absolute error of at most 0.50 mass-fraction percentage points and a passing one-sided exact sign test on the frozen roster (7 of 8 below target gives p = 0.035). Ties and abstentions count as failures. |
| External-calibration accuracy | Unmeasured | Apply the same rules with a median absolute error target of at most 0.75 mass-fraction percentage points. Pass the sign test and report signed bias. |
| Fraction assessed | Unmeasured for both methods | Return checked purity for at least 90% of eligible preparations per method. Keep abstentions, failures and preparations without runs in the denominator. Report counts by vendor and method. |
| Uncertainty agreement | Unmeasured for both methods | For at least 90% of scored preparations per method, require absolute error within the combined standard uncertainty of the error times a justified coverage factor. Include measurement and reference uncertainty and required covariance. Use `k = 2` only with a justified coverage basis. Any missing reference coverage, material uncertainty or required covariance leaves the criterion unverified. Report every miss. |
