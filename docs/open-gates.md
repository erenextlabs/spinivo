# Spinivo v1.0 open gates

Decision 7 calls for a limited preview if validation evidence is still missing. The owner decisions take precedence over the plan where they differ. The Nishizaki spectra and workbook comparisons are development evidence. The final accuracy test needs a separate public holdout. [docs/decisions.md](decisions.md), [docs/plan.md](plan.md)
